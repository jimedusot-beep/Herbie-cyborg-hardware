# Bill of materials

`components.csv` is a **planning inventory**, not proof of ownership or compatibility. Replace blank manufacturer part numbers only after selection. Status values: `owned-verified`, `donated-awaiting-delivery`, `requested`, `planned`, `optional`. Maintain exact voltage/current ratings, dimensions, and supplier evidence when known. Do not duplicate the authoritative BOM in the other repositories.

## Evidence ledger and intake guide

Use the existing [acquisition-evidence ledger](acquisition_evidence.csv) and [evidence policy](../docs/INVENTORY_EVIDENCE_POLICY.md) as the **single** source of provenance for any claimed owned/donated/requested parts. The [intake guide](INTAKE_GUIDE.md) explains how to assess candidate and salvaged electronics safely before creating a justified ledger/BOM record. No physical part is recorded as owned or received merely because it is described in a tutorial. Do not create a second inventory ledger.
